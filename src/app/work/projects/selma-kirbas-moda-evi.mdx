In this project, where I managed the brand's digital transformation process from end to end, I carried out the following works:

- **Web Design, Development & Performance Optimization**: I ensured the design and publication of a user-experience (UX) focused, mobile-friendly website that reflects the brand's aesthetic perception. I maximized the website's performance by making technical optimizations to ensure it runs at the highest speed and efficiency.
- **Search Engine Optimization (SEO)**: I implemented on-page and off-page SEO strategies to increase the website's visibility on search engines; I conducted keyword analysis and technical optimization processes.
- **Generative AI & Generative Engine Optimization (GEO & AEO)**: I planned and implemented Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) strategies to ensure the brand ranks high in next-generation AI-supported search engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) and voice search assistants.
- **Cybersecurity Protection**: I established the necessary security infrastructure (SSL, firewall, etc.) to protect the website against potential cyberattacks, malware, and bot traffic; I ensured the site remains securely online without interruption.
- **Digital Advertising Management**: I planned campaign strategies by conducting target audience analysis via Google Ads (Search and Display Network), optimized ad performance, and managed the budget.
