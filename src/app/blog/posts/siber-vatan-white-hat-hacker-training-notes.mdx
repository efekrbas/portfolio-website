## The Siber Vatan Journey
The Siber Vatan program is a great opportunity for talented young people who want to develop themselves in the field of cybersecurity. During this bootcamp, I had the chance to listen to both theoretical foundations and practical hacking methods from expert instructors in the field. In this article, I share the critical notes I took and the methodologies I learned during the camp.

## What Did We Learn?
During the training process, we dived deep into many topics such as Network Security, Web Application Vulnerabilities (OWASP Top 10), Cryptography, Reverse Engineering, and Open Source Intelligence (OSINT). In particular, the scenarios we encountered in applied CTF (Capture The Flag) competitions were very effective in translating theoretical knowledge into practice.

These notes serve as a roadmap for beginners in cybersecurity. You can check out the full article to read about the vision and technical details the Siber Vatan training added to me.
